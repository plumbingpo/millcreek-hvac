Kitchen Climate Control

Climate Control for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

Restaurants and food-service businesses have climate demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Kitchens generate enormous heat loads while dining rooms must stay comfortable, exhaust hoods pull out conditioned air that makeup air units must replace, and a busy Friday night pushes rooftop units to their limit. Millcreek HVAC specializes in food-service HVAC, including rooftop package units, split systems, correct tonnage and airflow sizing, makeup air balancing against hood exhaust, and scheduled service built around your hours.

Air balance is where many restaurant HVAC problems start. If a kitchen runs under negative pressure, it pulls dining-room air in the wrong direction, sucks hot or cold outdoor air through every door, and leaves staff miserable during the rush. Our technicians balance makeup air against hood exhaust and log every visit in written reports your landlord and auditors can review.

Replacement and maintenance plans are planned with owners, managers, and kitchen leads, so everything fits your operating schedule. If a job can't be done during service, we install during off-hours, keeping the kitchen running and the dining room comfortable throughout.

Safe Installation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. Our technicians inspect heat exchangers for cracks, confirm correct venting and combustion air, test electrical connections and disconnects, document the refrigerant charge, clear condensate lines, and check ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is simple: no carbon monoxide hazards, no poor air quality, no refrigerant leaks, and no ceiling tiles ruined by an overflowing drain. Findings go into a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

Regular professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and following through on our recommendations helps keep minor issues from becoming liabilities. It also builds a paper trail. We keep service logs, temperature and filter records, and a binder of reports for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or auditor asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.
